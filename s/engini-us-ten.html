Why this signal

Engini's named use cases are procure-to-pay, order-to-cash, IT service desk and HR task automation. A company advertising for humans to do exactly those jobs is telling you, in public and with a date on it, that the volume has outgrown the team.

The technology filter matters as much as the hiring one. Every company here already runs at least one of the four core systems Engini integrates with, so there is something to connect to on day one.

On the emails

Six of the ten sit on ordinary domains and verified cleanly. The other four sit on catch-all domains, where the mail server accepts every address and a “verified” status therefore proves nothing. Rather than hand those over with a warning attached, each was run through a second, unrelated provider. All four came back with the identical address. That does not prove the mailbox exists — nothing can, on a catch-all — but it removes the real risk, which is a single vendor guessing a naming pattern and being wrong.

What was measured
