Why this signal and not a job posting. I started by looking for open application security requisitions. That failed on the first company I checked: 737 live postings, no AppSec role in the forty most recent, and the same job duplicated eight times across cities. Job boards are noisy in a way that wastes your reps' time. A person who took the seat is a cleaner fact. It is also a better one for you, because the budget conversation follows the hire rather than preceding it.
What I could not confirm. The starting filter included companies indexed as running Cursor or GitHub Copilot. The enrichment step did not return that field, so I cannot independently verify AI coding-tool adoption at any of these ten and I have not claimed it on a single card. If the AI-generated-code angle matters to your pitch, treat it as a question to ask rather than a fact to open with.
