Ten sponsors that just took on new investors, from their own SEC filings.

Every one of these firms closed a raise in August and had to tell the SEC about it. Form D is a legal obligation, filed within 15 days of first sale, and it states the exact number of investors who came in. That number is the reporting burden Agora removes, published by the buyer, with a date on it.

Where this came from

1,193 Form D filings mentioning real estate were pulled from SEC EDGAR full-text search for 1–27 August 2026. Each one's raw XML was parsed for amount sold, investor count, date of first sale and the officers named in the filing. Filings under $2M or with fewer than 10 investors were dropped, as were institutional managers who build their own systems.

The filing names the fund vehicle. The buyer is the sponsor firm behind it. Mapping one to the other is the step that has to be done by hand, and it is why this list is not something a database can produce.

What this signal is not

It is not intent data and it is not a database field. Form D is a filing the sponsor is legally required to make, containing numbers they are legally required to state correctly. The cost of using it is that somebody has to read the filings. That is the entire moat.
