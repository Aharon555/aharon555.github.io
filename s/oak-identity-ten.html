What was removed, and why

Five identity vendors. Okta, Ping Identity, Entrust and Quest Software all matched the hiring signal and were cut — they sell identity, they do not buy it. Rubrik was cut late. It read as a clean security buyer until the contact search returned a Regional Director of Identity Sales and a Director of Product Marketing for Identity. Rubrik competes with Oak.

Staffing firms. Russell Tobin, Kforce, Randstad Digital, Compunnel, Aquent and Yoh all post IAM roles on behalf of clients. They are noise in every identity-hiring search, and they were filtered out by industry code rather than by hand.

One account with the best signal, dropped. Crusoe posted “Senior Systems Engineer, IAM Operations” on 12 August alongside three Managed-AI engineering roles — the sharpest AI-agent-identity story found anywhere in the search. It is not on this list because no reachable security leader exists in the record. A lead without a person is not a lead.

On the emails

Six of the ten sit on catch-all domains, flagged individually above. On a catch-all the mail server accepts every address, so a “verified” status carries no information. Large enterprises run catch-all far more often than mid-market companies, which is why this list carries a higher flag rate than a smaller-company list would.
